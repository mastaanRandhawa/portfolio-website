## Overview

Bistro Nova needed a mouth-watering online presence to drive reservations and showcase their seasonal menu to local diners and tourists alike.

## Challenge

They had no online menu, relied on third-party reservation platforms with high fees, and their Google presence was minimal compared to chain restaurants.

## Solution

We designed a visually rich restaurant landing page with dynamic menu displays, integrated reservation system, chef's story section, and optimized local SEO.
