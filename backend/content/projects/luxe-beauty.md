## Overview

Luxe Beauty Studio wanted to expand beyond their physical salon with a beautiful online store and integrated appointment booking system.

## Challenge

They had no e-commerce presence and were losing product sales to online competitors. Their booking process was entirely manual and error-prone.

## Solution

We created a stunning beauty e-commerce platform with curated product collections, seamless checkout, appointment scheduling, and a customer loyalty rewards program.
