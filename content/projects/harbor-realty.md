## Overview

Harbor Real Estate required a sophisticated property listing platform that positioned them as the premium choice in a competitive coastal market.

## Challenge

Listings were scattered across third-party platforms with inconsistent branding. The brokerage lacked a central hub to showcase properties and capture direct leads.

## Solution

We developed an elegant real estate website with MLS-integrated listings, interactive maps, virtual tour embeds, agent directories, and neighborhood market reports.
