## Overview

Wellness First Clinic needed a trustworthy, accessible website that made it easy for patients to learn about services and book appointments online.

## Challenge

The clinic relied on phone calls for scheduling, creating bottlenecks during peak hours. Their old site lacked HIPAA-conscious design patterns and failed accessibility standards.

## Solution

We created a calming, professional healthcare website with integrated appointment booking, service directories, provider profiles, and WCAG 2.1 AA compliant design throughout.
