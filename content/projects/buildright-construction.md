## Overview

BuildRight Construction needed a website that showcased their craftsmanship and made it effortless for potential clients to request project quotes.

## Challenge

Their previous site had no project portfolio, poor mobile experience, and no way for visitors to submit quote requests online — losing business to competitors.

## Solution

We built a robust construction portfolio site with filterable project galleries, detailed case studies, team profiles, and a multi-step quote request form with automated notifications.
