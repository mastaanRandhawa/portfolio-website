## Overview

BC Tigers FC needed a single hub to run their annual soccer tournaments — most recently the 14th Annual Miri Piri Soccer Tournament — where players, coaches, and fans could follow every match as it happened. The platform pulls together fixtures, live scores, standings, and knockout brackets for multiple competitions in one place.

## Challenge

Running a multi-day tournament across several parks means dozens of matches, constantly changing scores, and coaches who need rosters and schedules at their fingertips. The club needed a fast, mobile-first system that could serve live data to hundreds of spectators without becoming a maintenance burden for organizers.

## Solution

We built a full-stack web application with a React and Vite front end backed by a Node REST API. It surfaces live matches, recent results, upcoming fixtures, standings, and knockout brackets, plus tournament announcements, downloadable roster templates, and user accounts — all updated in real time as scores come in from the field.
