## Overview

Lumière Salon is a luxury hair studio offering precision cuts, hand-painted balayage, gloss treatments, and bridal styling. The site was designed to feel like a fashion editorial — intimate, elegant, and effortlessly premium.

## Challenge

Upscale salons compete on perception. The brand needed a site that signalled runway-level expertise, communicated services and pricing with clarity, and guided visitors toward booking an appointment — while feeling calm and considered rather than busy.

## Solution

We built a refined React site pairing Playfair Display and Inter for an editorial voice, with generous imagery and whitespace. Each service is presented with pricing and duration, an "Our Studio" section builds trust, and prominent "Book Appointment" calls-to-action carry the visitor toward conversion across every screen size.
