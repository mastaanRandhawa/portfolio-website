## Overview

Bao Bei is a modern Chinese brasserie in the heart of Vancouver's Chinatown, serving shareable small plates influenced by Taiwan and China. The site captures that intimate, editorial dining feel while making it easy for guests to explore the menu and book a table.

## Challenge

A destination restaurant needs its website to convey atmosphere before a guest ever walks in. The design had to feel warm and elegant, put the food front and centre, surface press and awards for credibility, and drive reservations — all without overwhelming the visitor.

## Solution

We built an elegant React site with a moody, photography-led hero and a serif-driven type system that mirrors the brasserie's identity. Sections walk guests through the story, a sampled menu, press recognition (including a Michelin Guide recommendation), and the Family Table group experience, with clear reservation calls-to-action throughout and full hours and location details.
