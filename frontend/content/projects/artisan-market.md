## Overview

Artisan Market launched as an online marketplace connecting local artisans with customers seeking unique, handcrafted products.

## Challenge

They needed a multi-vendor e-commerce platform that was easy for artisans to manage while providing shoppers with a premium browsing and buying experience.

## Solution

We developed a full-featured marketplace with vendor dashboards, advanced product search and filtering, wishlist functionality, and a streamlined multi-vendor checkout system.
