## Overview

AK Wraps & Customs is a premier auto protection and customization studio in Delta, BC. The site positions the shop as the go-to specialist in Greater Vancouver — from daily drivers to exotics — with an aesthetic that matches the premium nature of the work.

## Challenge

A wrap and detailing studio lives or dies on how its craftsmanship looks online. The brand needed a site that felt as high-end as the vehicles it works on, loaded quickly despite rich visuals, and made it effortless for prospective clients to browse services and request a quote.

## Solution

We built a dark, cinematic single-page site with Three.js 3D elements and smooth scroll animations, backed by Bootstrap for a fully responsive layout. Seven services are laid out clearly, a portfolio grid highlights completed builds, and a structured "Start My Signature Build" enquiry form captures leads. Lazy loading and performance tuning keep the experience fast on every device.
