## Overview

Apex Automotive Group needed a modern digital showroom to compete with national dealership chains. We built a fast, mobile-first website with real-time inventory integration and streamlined lead capture.

## Challenge

Their outdated website was slow, not mobile-friendly, and failed to capture leads effectively. Potential buyers were leaving for competitor sites with better online experiences.

## Solution

We designed and developed a premium automotive website with advanced inventory filtering, virtual showroom tours, financing calculators, and optimized contact forms — all built for speed and conversions.
